This installer does not include Skylanders: SuperChargers or any of its data.

You will need your own legal copy of the Xbox 360 disc, either as an ISO file or an
already-extracted folder. Two European (PAL) discs are supported: the English / German one
(the one to use) and the Spanish / Italian one. The next step checks this automatically.

This is an alpha build and may contain bugs, crashes, or other issues.
